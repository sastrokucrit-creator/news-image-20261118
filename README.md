# 18 November 2026 — the day in one image

A front-page poster summing up the world's news of Wednesday 18 November
2026, for the make.fun bounty `news-image-20261118`.

The lead story is the US–Iran escalation overnight: the US destroyed five
Iranian oil tankers in the Gulf of Oman, and Iran hit back near the Strait
of Hormuz and fired missiles at US troops in Jordan. The masthead names the
day — **18 NOVEMBER 2026** — and five labelled strips carry the day's other
stories: Ukraine striking the Kerch Bridge for a second night, the UN's
winter warning for Gaza, COP31's decisive phase in Antalya, the Quad summit
in Manila, and Eisenkot being tapped to form Israel's next government.

`main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only) copies `entry.png` to
`/output/entry.png` (or `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR`). The image is a 1024×1024
PNG of 666,395 bytes; no network is used, so two runs write identical bytes.
